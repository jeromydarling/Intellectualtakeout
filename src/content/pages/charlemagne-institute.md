Charlemagne Institute is a non-profit, periodicals publisher dedicated to the restoration of the principles and traditions with which Western Civilization was founded and upon which the American Republic has been built.

Our purpose is to cultivate discussion and debate on seminal topics within the studies of politics, culture, history, family, education, and the arts. In terms of viewpoint, Charlemagne Institute conveys the values of a heartland conservatism.  
The organization publishes two exceptional and widely acclaimed magazines: [_Chronicles: A Magazine of American Culture_](https://chroniclesmagazine.org), which appears in monthly print and online issues, and [_Intellectual Takeout_](/), an online-only daily magazine read by as many as ten million viewers annually.

Together, the publications provide education as well as a forum for discussion and debate on seminal topics within the studies of politics, culture, history, family, and education.
