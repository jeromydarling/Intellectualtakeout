_Intellectual Takeout_ is a 501(c)(3) non-profit with the mission of feeding minds and pursuing truth. Our leadership team is listed below.

**Devin Foley, CEO** – After a short time away from the organization, Devin Foley is once again CEO of Intellectual Takeout. Growing up in rural San Diego, he aimed to be a pilot or go into politics — his career reflects the latter path, though many a day he wishes he were flying. Foley graduated from Hillsdale College in 2001 and shortly after married his wife, Nicole, of nearly 25 years. Together they have seven children and live in Stillwater, Minnesota.

In 2001, Foley joined Center of the American Experiment, a conservative, Minnesota-based think tank. He quickly rose in the institution and became the director of development. Additionally, he gained experience during those years working with Governor Tim Pawlenty and Secretary of State Mary Kiffmeyer. In 2009, he had the opportunity to co-found Intellectual Takeout as a stand-alone organization and led Intellectual Takeout as its president until 2022 when board changes caused him to take a sabbatical from the organization. In 2025, the board of directors brought Foley back as CEO and he looks forward to the next chapter of this culture-changing institution.

**Annie Holmquist, Editor in Chief** – Annie Holmquist is back as the editor-in-chief for _Intellectual Takeout!_ She launched her writing and editing career with the institution in 2009 as an intern and eventually became the editor-in-chief of the digital publication. Beloved for her writing, wit, and wisdom, _Intellectual Takeout’s_ board of directors and CEO were thrilled to bring her back into her leadership position in 2025. Annie’s writing may be found at _The Epoch Times_, _American Essence Magazine_, _1819 News,_ _Alpha News, Opportunity for All Kids,_ _Foundation for Economic Education,_ and her Substack, _Annie’s Atti_c.
