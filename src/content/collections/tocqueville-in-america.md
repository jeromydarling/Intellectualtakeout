Alexis de Tocqueville toured the United States for nine months and wrote the
most enduring diagnosis of the American character ever put to paper. These
essays return to *Democracy in America* again and again — on equality and
envy, individualism and conformity, statesmen and their absence — and find
that the Frenchman keeps being right about us.
