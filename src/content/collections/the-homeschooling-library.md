Homeschooling went from fringe experiment to mainstream movement in the span of
a generation — and Intellectual Takeout covered every step. This collection
gathers the essential pieces: the teachers who left the classroom to teach
their own children, the professors who changed their minds about homeschooled
students, and the quiet legal battles over whether parents will keep the
freedom to choose.
