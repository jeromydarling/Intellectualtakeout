Colleges have massive racial and ideological double standards. A left-wing employee can get away with calling for [the castration of white men](https://www.yahoo.com/news/georgetown-condemns-disrespectful-discourse-professor-154051029.html), while a conservative employee at the very same university gets investigated [and suspended merely for criticizing](https://www.thecollegefix.com/georgetown-suspends-scholar-for-tweet-against-bidens-affirmative-action-supreme-court-nomination-process/) affirmative action.

The latest example is from the [University of California](https://equity.ucla.edu/who-we-are/) at Los Angeles, where its [Director of Race and Equity](https://johnathansperkins.com/), Johnathan Perkins, has [claimed](https://twitter.com/JohnathanPerk/status/1701031947349405840) that “every white person is racist to some degree”, and falsely claimed that “white people cannot be victims of racism,” and “only white people can impose racist harm.”

These positions conflict with the civil-rights responsibilities of the office where Perkins works, UCLA’s Office of Diversity, Equity & Inclusion.

Under the civil-rights laws, white people can be victims of racial discrimination, as the courts have ruled many times. Perkins is simply wrong. Moreover, non-whites have been found guilty of imposing racist harm, and stereotyping whites as racist simply because they are white is considered racially hostile workplace behavior that can lead to civil-rights violations. The Supreme Court ruled that it [was illegal](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/476/267) to fire white employees because they were white, in _Wygant v. Jackson Board of Education_ [and in](https://scholar.google.com/scholar_case?case=10457097098806271127&hl=en&as_sdt=6&as_vis=1&oi=scholarr) _McDonald v. Santa Fe Trail Transportation Co._ (1976). Courts have ruled that racial harassment is illegal even when it is committed by blacks against whites, in cases such as [_Huckabay v. Moore_](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/us-5th-circuit/1097623.html) (1998) and [_Bowen v. Missouri Department of Social Services_](https://scholar.google.com/scholar_case?case=1496897331356060121&hl=en&as_sdt=6&as_vis=1&oi=scholarr) (2002).

The federal courts have [sentenced non-whites](https://scholar.google.com/scholar_case?case=9760986904087856071&hl=en&as_sdt=6&as_vis=1&oi=scholarr) to prison for committing violent racial harms — hate crimes — against whites. Those decisions make plain that it is not “only white people” who “can impose racist harm,” as Perkins claims. Courts have ruled that “baseless accusations of racism” made against white employees because they are white can be racial harassment that contributes to a racially hostile work environment in violation of the 1964 Civil Rights Act. (_S__ee_ [_Underwood v. Northport Health Services_](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp2/57/1289/2417228/) (1989)).

College administrators can be removed from their positions when they take racist positions at odds with their job responsibilities. There is no right to be racist while holding a civil-rights position. (_See [Harleston v. Jeffries](https://scholar.google.com/scholar_case?case=9760986904087856071&hl=en&as_sdt=6&as_vis=1&oi=scholarr)_ (1995)).

Yet Perkins advocates racial discrimination against whites, and frequently expresses racial hostility, with impunity. He [tells](https://libertyunyielding.com/2023/06/23/university-equity-director-says-whites-should-work-for-free-on-juneteenth/) whites not to wish him a “Happy Juneteenth” [because he will “flip tf out”](https://twitter.com/JohnathanPerk/status/1670536709644365824) if they do. He [says](https://twitter.com/JohnathanPerk/status/1670899302108119040) that [whites](https://www.thecollegefix.com/ucla-diversity-official-white-people-should-work-for-free-on-juneteenth/), unlike Blacks, “should NOT be awarded the day off for Juneteenth,” even though that is discriminatory. Perkins [says](https://twitter.com/JohnathanPerk/status/1670904872932368386) that [while](https://www.thecollegefix.com/ucla-diversity-official-white-people-should-work-for-free-on-juneteenth/) “most” people “value community,” “white people do not.” He [said](https://twitter.com/JohnathanPerk/status/1670838234602151938) on Juneteenth 2023 that [he was](https://www.thecollegefix.com/ucla-diversity-official-white-people-should-work-for-free-on-juneteenth/) “Blaming white people, who should ALL be working today, of all damn days.”

In 2022, Perkins [reacted](https://www.thecollegefix.com/the-world-hated-that-woman-university-officials-celebrate-the-death-of-queen-elizabeth-ii/) to the death of England’s Queen Elizabeth [by saying](https://libertyunyielding.com/2023/06/23/university-equity-director-says-whites-should-work-for-free-on-juneteenth/),“Good riddance.” He also publicly [yearned](https://www.thecollegefix.com/ucla-diversity-official-white-people-should-work-for-free-on-juneteenth/) for the death of Supreme Court Justice Clarence Thomas, saying on Twitter that “no one wants to openly admit \[we all\] hope Clarence Thomas dies.”

Perkins undignified, hateful rants are at odds with the stated “[mission](https://equity.ucla.edu/who-we-are/)” of UCLA’s Office of Diversity, Equity & Inclusion, where Perkins is a director. It describes its “mission” as “to uphold dignity for all.”

Perkins has wrongly [claimed](https://libertyunyielding.com/2023/06/23/university-equity-director-says-whites-should-work-for-free-on-juneteenth/) that “white people” “murder Black people in droves.” As a black attorney [noted](https://www.nationalreview.com/corner/flames-from-false-narratives/) in the _National Review__,_ “blacks aren’t being ‘hunted’ by whites.” Indeed, “‘Between 2012 and 2015, blacks committed 85.5 percent of all black-white interracial violent victimizations.’”

Perkins previously worked in Harvard University’s Office of General Counsel. Perkins [fabricated](https://www.thecollegefix.com/ucla-diversity-official-white-people-should-work-for-free-on-juneteenth/) a racial incident in 2011, claiming to be racially harassed by campus cops at the University of Virginia — as a way to “bring attention to the topic of police misconduct.”

“As I stood there, humiliated, with my hands on the police car, my only thought was: ‘There is nothing I can do to right this wrong. I have absolutely no recourse,’” Perkins wrote in a letter to the editor to the student newspaper, the Cavalier Daily. But after an investigation found his claims to be false, [he was forced to admit](https://www.facebook.com/notes/964739540685101/) the events he claimed happened “did not occur.”

Last year, Perkins accused those who took issue with his argument for abolishing the police of engaging in “[white supremacy](https://campusreform.org/article?id=19404).”

Perkins claims to be “[antiracist](https://johnathansperkins.com/).” But self-described “antiracists” are often racist. Earlier this year, Auburn University’s Black Student Union sent a document containing [over 250 racial slurs](https://libertyunyielding.com/2023/02/13/black-student-union-circulates-racial-slurs-for-white-people/) for whites in a recent GroupMe chat it hosted, even though it has claimed to support “antiracism.” The Black Student Union (BSU) is [part of](https://involve.auburn.edu/governance/) Auburn’s student Governance Branch along with the Student Government Association.

The insults for white people [included](https://libertyunyielding.com/2023/02/13/black-student-union-circulates-racial-slurs-for-white-people/) “mayonnaise monsters,” “failed abortions,” “deformed dna,” “mockery of humanity,” “bleach demons,” “albino pigs,” and “not meant to exist.”

If white students behaved this way toward blacks, they would likely be disciplined by a college for racial harassment (whatever the First Amendment may say). But since this was aimed at whites by blacks, no college administrator cares. Legally, though, blacks can be found liable for racially harassing whites. For example, courts have ruled that employers are liable for allowing racial harassment against whites, in cases like _[Bowen v. Missouri Department of Social Services](http://caselaw.findlaw.com/us-8th-circuit/1113537.html)_ (2002) and _[Huckabay v. Moore](http://openjurist.org/137/f3d/871/huckabay-v-moore)_ (1998)).

In practice, “Anti-racism” means left-wing ideology, including opposition to capitalism and support for illegal racial preferences. “To love capitalism is to end up loving racism. To love racism is to end up loving capitalism…Capitalism is essentially racist; racism is essentially capitalist,” [says the](https://twitter.com/BOLMOJOLA/status/1366288133483069440) best-selling “anti-racist” book _How to Be An Antiracist_. That book [is a](https://twitter.com/sullydish/status/1414952671384047628) “comprehensive introduction to critical race theory,” [gushes](https://t.co/YsY4VkvLRg?amp=1) the leading progressive media organ _[Slate](https://t.co/YsY4VkvLRg?amp=1)_.

The “[key concept](https://legalinsurrection.com/2020/07/harrison-bergeron-university/)” in the book _How to Be an Antiracist_ is that discrimination against whites is the only way to achieve equality: “The only remedy to past discrimination is present discrimination. The only remedy to present discrimination is future discrimination,” [it says](https://legalinsurrection.com/2020/07/harrison-bergeron-university/). Such discrimination violates Supreme Court rulings like [_Wygant v. Jackson Board of Education_](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/476/267) (1986).
