The red flags started to fly on the COVID-19 vax for Dr. Renata Moon in 2021. She’d been watching the data on the vaccines, and then she watched the hearings on the vaccine being used in children.

“While watching the hearings, I was really appalled,” Dr. Moon said, adding that when she heard Dr. Eric Rubin of the FDA’s Vaccines and Related Biological Products Advisory Committee admit the FDA didn’t know how the vax would affect children, she was alarmed. “He said \[that\] we don’t have the data \[and that\] we won’t know \[if the vax is safe\] until we roll it out.”

Nonetheless, that unproven vaccine was approved for use on children.

“How can we be mandating an experimental product with unknown long-term safety and a lot of short-term safety signals? We know the vaccines don’t stop the transmission or spread of the disease. The vaccine mandates were unethical.” Dr. Moon said.

A board-certified pediatrician with over 20 years practicing, Dr. Moon has never recommended the COVID-19 vax for her patients. And up until recently she trusted the advice of regulatory agencies regarding vaccinations.

“I am not anti-vax. But this one is different,” she said.

So different that she spoke out at the risk of her employment. And in December 2021, despite a clean history of caring well for patients, she was fired from her job. Her employer wanted to know if she’d gotten the vax, and when she wouldn’t tell them, she was fired.

“I pulled out the Nuremburg Code to remind myself what it says—because this vaccine is a medical experiment. It’s unethical to force people to undergo medical experimentation,” Dr. Moon said. “And if an employer mandates what you have to do with your personal health care, you have become a modern-day slave. I said, ‘No,’ and they pulled me from a fully booked clinic.”

But despite the repercussions, Dr. Moon has continued to speak out, even if it could mean losing her job again.

“If we don’t all speak out, we are going to lose everything,” she said. “We are going to completely lose our freedom. We must stand with the many millions of others who are opposed to this.”

“I took an oath to ‘do no harm,’” she said. “As the data rolled out on the vaccine and COVID-19, it became clear that children had basically a zero risk of death from infection by COVID. They have potential serious risk from taking the COVID-19 shots. These shots are dangerous, and some people have died from taking them. They need to be pulled from the market immediately. If I lose my career over this, I will give it all up as long as we stay free as a nation and fix this catastrophe. Our regulatory agencies are not acting in the best interest of the American people. Silencing and threatening the many concerned physicians who are speaking out is clearly not the right answer.”

Dr. Moon’s unique family background has informed her decision to speak out: Her parents grew up under communism in the country formerly known as Czechoslovakia.

“My parents were new immigrants, and I grew up a proud, freedom-loving American,” she said. “As a kid I traveled to behind the Iron Curtain (to what is now the Czech Republic). The way my relatives and others were living under communism was awful. Every aspect of their lives was controlled by their communist government. There is a reason that millions of people have fled from communism—these horrible systems never work.”

So, when the pandemic and forced masks and mandated vaccines happened, Dr. Moon recognized the rising authoritarianism.

“The pandemic and masks and vax mandates was all pure communism,” she said. “My family were tracked back then—you had to sign in with the village police just to visit relatives 60 kilometers away. I saw how they were living under communism: terrible lives.”

But it wasn’t just the draconian nature of the vaccine mandates that concerned her—it was the many connections to being harmed from taking the vaccines. While many critics of the COVID-19 vaccines take issue with the mRNA platform on which these vaccines are based, that’s not the only problem, according to Dr. Moon.

“I’m highly concerned that the lipid nanoparticles themselves—minus the mRNA—are dangerous,” she said. “And they are trying to use the same lipid nanoparticle mRNA platform in many other vaccines.”

As a pediatrician, Dr. Moon is especially concerned about how these vaccines are impacting children. She said one West Coast mother was forced by her state government to have her kids get the booster shots. And this judge’s decision occurred even though kids have basically no risk of death by COVID-19 infection.

“Kids have a statistical zero percent risk for COVID-19 fatality. Yet the vaccine is now on the traditional childhood immunization protocol, and more schools may soon be mandating it,” she said.

It’s unclear how many pediatricians in the U.S. are now also eyeing the vax critically, but that number is growing.

“We have a group of concerned pediatricians—word of mouth only,” Dr. Moon said. “More and more, physicians are realizing something is very wrong.”

While the U.S. government [has admitted that myocarditis](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/covid-19/clinical-considerations/myocarditis.html) is an adverse effect of the vax (especially among male adolescents and young men), as a pediatrician, Dr. Moon now has direct knowledge of an unusual number of [myocarditis](https://www.statnews.com/2023/05/05/myocarditis-covid-vaccines-study/#:~:text=In%20the%20highest%2Drisk%20group,for%20Disease%20Control%20and%20Prevention.) cases and other adverse reactions to the shot.

And the myocarditis reaction to a supposedly safe vaccine is only one sign of how corrupted so many of our interconnected systems have become: the authoritarianism of the vax mandates; the monitoring and squelching of free speech by the government and social media companies; the silencing of critics of the vaccine and other restrictive measures linked to the virus; and the compromised state of the U.S. government, the World Health Organization, and the medical establishment.

“These systems are completely compromised,” Dr. Moon said. “We are experiencing the collapse of our health care systems in the midst of a Communist/Marxist revolution.”

Even little kids are under attack through disruptions in their daily lives: forced vaccination and masking, constant fearmongering by authorities, and hateful indoctrination in schools.

“It’s caused extreme emotional distress for kids,” said Dr. Moon. “They are trying to destroy our nation by destroying our nation’s children.”

But despite this, Dr. Moon still has hope.

“I believe we are in a battle with evil and that God is with us,” she said. “But He asks that we do everything we can to fight this.”

The message from many of those concerned with these mandated medical experiments and rollbacks of our God-given rights as Americans is that it’s fourth quarter of this game, and we all must keep hitting hard and tackling mean. The game is not over, and we will prevail if we hang tough, fight hard, and stick together.

“Ronald Reagan said \[that\] we’re one generation away from losing our freedom,” Dr. Moon said. “We are Americans, and we are tough—we have to peacefully fight and overcome this. We are fighting for a meaningful future for our nation’s children.”
