**1\. (Above)** We’re not all the same. Everybody knows that we’re not all the same with the exception of—apparently—public school administrators and curriculum writers.

![](https://chroniclesmagazine-legacy.ae-admin.com/assets/3/28/college_trade_school.jpg)

**2.** The condescension is palpable today, even if it’s not based on any evidence.

Fact: college isn’t for everyone, and there are good options for those who choose not to get a four-year degree.

![](https://chroniclesmagazine-legacy.ae-admin.com/assets/3/28/debt_0.jpg)

**3.** Speaking of college, does it seem like the tuition costs of a four-year degree are out of control?

Probably because [they are](http://nces.ed.gov/fastfacts/display.asp?id=76).

![](https://chroniclesmagazine-legacy.ae-admin.com/assets/3/28/droupout_rate.jpg)

**4.** Sure, the dropout rate as a whole has gotten a bit better over the past decade, but try telling that to parents of students in larger cities like [Los Angeles, where the dropout rate is 17%,](http://dq.cde.ca.gov/dataquest/CohortRates/CRByProgram.aspx?Agg=D&Topic=Dropouts&TheYear=2013-14&cds=19647330000000&RC=District&SubGroup=Ethnic/Racial) or [Chicago, where the dropout rate is more than 25%](http://cps.edu/SchoolData/Pages/SchoolData.aspx)!

![](https://chroniclesmagazine-legacy.ae-admin.com/assets/3/28/cartoon_public_school.jpg)

**5.** Does it sometimes feel like we’re wasting what President Kennedy called “our fundamental resource” by filling heads with multiple choice bubbles and scantrons? 

![](https://chroniclesmagazine-legacy.ae-admin.com/assets/3/28/throwmoney.png)

**6.** And finally, the go-to, fix-all solution to any problem in education: throw money at it.

Over the past 40 years, [education funding](http://object.cato.org/sites/cato.org/files/pubs/pdf/pa746.pdf) has tripled, while math and verbal skills have [_decreased_](http://washington.cbslocal.com/2014/04/07/study-no-link-between-school-spending-student-achievement/) by three percent.  

Students and parents need real, tangible change. Not just more money.
