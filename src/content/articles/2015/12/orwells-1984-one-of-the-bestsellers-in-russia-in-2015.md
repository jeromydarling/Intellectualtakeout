Under the leadership of Vladimir Putin, some have [accused](http://www.themoscowtimes.com/opinion/article/russia-s-new-totalitarianism-depends-on-silence/519659.html) Russia of slipping back toward [totalitarianism](http://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2015/aug/12/miklos-radvanyi-vladimir-putins-russian-bear-a-tot/).

Which is why some news outlets see significance in the fact that George Orwell’s famous dystopian novel _1984_ was one of [the bestselling books](http://style.rbc.ru/news/art/2015/12/23/22467/) in Russia this past year.  

Paul Gobel at the blog [Window on Eurasia](http://windowoneurasia2.blogspot.com/2015/12/a-bakers-dozen-of-neglected-russian_25.html) writes,

> “Russians purchased more copies of George Orwell’s dystopian novel, ‘1984,’ in 2015 than all but a handful of other books. Some may have bought it because of its warnings about what happens when big media trumps everything else; others in contrast may have seen it as a road map for their future under Vladimir Putin.”

And here is [Breitbart](http://www.breitbart.com/big-government/2015/12/29/orwells-1984-one-top-ten-bestsellers-2015-russia/#disqus_thread) on the matter:

> “That the Russians have turned to Orwell is quite telling. They know totalitarianism when they see it.”

Interesting. However, Americans are by no means immune from the same hasty conclusion, as _1984_ also remains one of the [bestsellers](http://money.cnn.com/2013/06/12/news/1984-nsa-snowden/index.html) in the literature category on Amazon.

Based on bestseller lists, Americans may also want to take into account [Aldous Huxley’s warning](http://www.informationclearinghouse.info/article31319.htm) in his dystopian novel _Brave New World_: that “non-stop distractions of the most fascinating nature are deliberately used as instruments of policy, for the purpose of preventing people from paying too much attention to the realities of the social and political situation.”

I notice that 5 of the [current top 10 bestsellers](http://www.amazon.com/best-sellers-books-Amazon/zgbs/books/ref=pd_dp_ts_b_1) on Amazon are adult coloring books…
