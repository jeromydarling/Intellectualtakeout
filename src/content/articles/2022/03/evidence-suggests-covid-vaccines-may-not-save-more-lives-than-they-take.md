Medical journals and textbooks are clear that the only way to accurately determine the life-or-death impacts of medical treatments is by measuring “all-cause mortality” in “randomized controlled trials.” Clinical lingo aside, this is simply the number of deaths in studies where people are randomly assigned to receive or not receive a certain treatment.

Though widely ignored in media coverage of COVID-19 vaccines, medical journals describe all-cause mortality in randomized controlled trials (RCTs) as:

*   “the most objective outcome” (_[Journal of Critical Care](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0883944121001143)_)
*   “the most relevant outcome” (_[The Lancet Respiratory Medicine](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2213260021003313)_)
*   “the most significant outcome” (_[JAMA Internal Medicine](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/article-abstract/486707)_)
*   “the most important outcome” (_[PLoS Medicine](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1002958)_)
*   “the most important outcome” (_[Journal of the National Medical Association](https://www.proquest.com/openview/921fac3348bf38ddaf8a4c32359d69e8/1?pq-origsite=gscholar&cbl=36588)_)
*   “the most important outcome” (_[International Journal of Cardiology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167527316303205)_)

Beyond the fact that death is the most severe and clearest health outcome, the reason why this measure is more vital than any other is because RCTs [control](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3196997/) for every possible confounding factor, including those that are not obvious. Thus, a clinical [research methods guide](https://obgyn.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1471-0528.15199) states that RCTs are the “gold standard” for research because they provide “a rigorous tool to examine cause–effect,” which “is not possible with any other study design.”

Combined with the use of a placebo so that people don’t alter their mindsets or behaviors as a result of knowing they received the treatment, quality RCTs ensure that any significant difference in the total number of deaths among the people who receive and don’t receive a treatment is, in fact, [caused by the treatment](https://obgyn.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1471-0528.15199). This eliminates subjective judgments about the root causes of death, which is a major point of contention with C-19 vaccines.

Unlike other data which can be easily manipulated through [statistical tampering](https://www.justfacts.com/immigration#crime_media_statistics), all-cause mortality in RCTs is straightforward and solid. If an RCT is large enough and properly conducted, a [simple tally](https://obgyn.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1471-0528.15199) of all deaths among people who receive and don’t receive a treatment proves whether the treatment saves more lives than it takes.

Underscoring all of the above facts, medical textbooks and journals explain that:

*   [RCTs](https://www.amazon.com/Rutherfords-Vascular-Surgery-2-Set/dp/1455753041) are “the pinnacle in clinical design.”
*   [RCTs](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3196997/) are “the best way to study the safety and efficacy of new treatments.”
*   “the act of randomisation in a large” [RCT](https://obgyn.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1471-0528.15199) “balances participant characteristics (both observed and unobserved) between the groups, allowing attribution of any differences in outcome to the intervention.”

In this case, the “intervention” is FDA-approved COVID vaccines, and the “outcome” is death. That vital data was gathered in RCTs involving [72,663](https://www.justfacts.com/reference/covid-19_mrna_vaccines_mortality.xls) adults and older children for the Moderna and Pfizer vaccines. However, the FDA presented these results in a place and manner likely to be overlooked, and no [major](https://www.google.com/search?q=Covid-19+%22Pfizer%22+%2221+deaths%22+%22vaccine%22+%2217%22+%22placebo%22&num=100&lr=&safe=images&hl=en&biw=1024&bih=654&source=lnt&tbs=cdr%3A1%2Ccd_min%3A7%2F1%2F2021%2Ccd_max%3A2%2F22%2F2022&tbm=nws) media [outlet](https://www.google.com/search?q=Covid-19+%22Moderna%22+%2216+deaths%22+%22vaccine%22+%22placebo%22&num=100&lr=&safe=images&hl=en&biw=1536&bih=1028&tbs=cdr%3A1%2Ccd_min%3A7%2F1%2F2021%2Ccd_max%3A2%2F22%2F2022&tbm=nws&ei=sLYWYu-4BJ6yqtsPw4C_qAY&ved=0ahUKEwjv0OCo8Zb2AhUemWoFHUPAD2UQ4dUDCAw&uact=5&oq=Covid-19+%22Moderna%22+%2216+deaths%22+%22vaccine%22+%22placebo%22&gs_lcp=Cgxnd3Mtd2l6LW5ld3MQAzIFCCEQoAEyBQghEKsCMgUIIRCrAlCYC1iYC2DUDmgAcAB4AIABcIgB3AKSAQMyLjKYAQCgAQKgAQHAAQE&sclient=gws-wiz-news) has covered [them](https://www.justfacts.com/reference/covid-19_mrna_vaccines_mortality.xls).

The results reveal that [70](https://www.justfacts.com/reference/covid-19_mrna_vaccines_mortality.xls) people died during the Moderna and Pfizer trials, including [37](https://www.justfacts.com/reference/covid-19_mrna_vaccines_mortality.xls) who received Covid vaccines and [33](https://www.justfacts.com/reference/covid-19_mrna_vaccines_mortality.xls) who did not. Combined with the fact that [half](https://www.justfacts.com/reference/covid-19_mrna_vaccines_mortality.xls) of the study participants were given vaccinations and the other half were given placebos, these crucial results provide no indication that the vaccines save more lives than they take.

Accounting for sampling margins of error—as is common for medical journals and uncommon for the media—the results demonstrate with [95 percent confidence](https://www.justfacts.com/reference/covid-19_mrna_vaccines_mortality.xls) that:

*   neither of the vaccines decreased or increased the absolute risk of death by any more than 0.08 percent over the course of the trials.
*   the vaccines could prevent up to two deaths or cause up to three deaths per year among every 1,000 people.

Importantly, those results:

*   apply to adults and older children averaged as a group, and the vaccines’ benefits and risks can vary considerably for each individual.
*   don’t apply beyond the timeframes of the studies, which were limited to several months.
*   don’t apply to people who were excluded from the studies, including those who are severely ill, previously had COVID-19, or have an immune disorder like HIV.
*   don’t apply to the currently dominant SARS-CoV-2 variant (Omicron).
