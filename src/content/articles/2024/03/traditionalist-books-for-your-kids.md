Your kids are (finally!) reading on their own . . . Now what?

Well, now is when we parents face the often-herculean task of trying to verify which books are safe and fit for our kids to read—not to mention supportive of traditional values!

I’d like to offer a list of some favorite young adult books that have been read and approved by four solidly traditionalist parents: my husband, my mother, my mother-in-law. I also have been a librarian in both public libraries and private schools, and with this experience, I’ll add for convenience a suggested age range, a short summary of why the book qualifies as traditional, and any relevant notes for parents.

Much like [my last list](/2023/10/25-traditionalist-books-to-read-with-your-children/), most of these are hidden gems few readers will come across in conventional bookstores or literary circles.

1\. _[A Squire’s Tale](https://www.amazon.com/Squires-Tale-Tales/dp/061873743X/ref=sr_1_2?crid=15LUEFC5RSYBO&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.VwhP9jPlZXFQz3jkScAYRuvBx16LMyBo-rx2RdP1hKvr1UrC7AZIcG793f8IxrLvvFF8_2SCjVkTsm8T2uC-H6vopVZVdwFwxLIcJc8tkNU7BhQe2TDWEVLop2kIk-TZu-ty00ZSnqvIAYe8wBlncaPiClECkBGnmi4-MH4ugi8J4lwId3WUR_4EjeYSo3O1ZhDVQ3Hhuea3KQyQY7cmN5OGi5DUmsjL-lBZQ6BYH4g.7LFbLJnhjcwmcD1e839INR8KwQBNOYDVJ2gWL0xrnds&dib_tag=se&keywords=a+squire%27s+tale+set&qid=1709000262&sprefix=a+squire%27s+tale+s%2Caps%2C245&sr=8-2)_ series by Gerald Morris (12+). All the King Arthur legends are retold in a relatable and comedic voice in Morris’ 10-book series—many through the eyes of a squire named Terence. Themes include loyalty, honor, courage, and creativity. (Favorites of mine include _The Lioness and Her Knight_, _The Savage Damsel_ _and the Dwarf_, and _The Ballad of Sir Dinadan_.)

2\. The _Mitchells_ series by Hilda Van Stockum (8+). This trilogy features _[The Mitchells: Five for Victory](https://www.amazon.com/Five-for-Victory-Hilda-Van-Stockum-audio/dp/B01HQO2RWQ/ref=sr_1_1?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.52qYfb88uRGQHmk5y7TfwEN6IZdBWh1JRxrq8koc--prDIcHTtXaE41dPfB1pemgy5YGalISsYHW_5jvasqaumU54ZkGNV-Sr4LIVnBTU0ol5c9dpOhhdcVqGi_iwZFVmyE7JxVKUrhZrGM5iju-ns0TBHu3Oryne77awNHTj96zcv-fcNqZ_6-X66UHN8PQM6NicdMxsMki0RTCHLpU1RZ2CTHTeU0C3Cq1DRR1I1U.kEzvPqhTrfmw4RwUAmaBdrcokCDCp6cdqkcJNVAJEeM&dib_tag=se&keywords=the+mitchells+series&qid=1708994876&sr=8-1)_, _[A Canadian Summer](https://www.amazon.com/Canadian-Summer-Hilda-Van-Stockum-audiobook/dp/B01M3VHT1A/ref=sr_1_4?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.lSltNmEfnS0tcen9H3hwSVoFID3vr_IQNoVmgbqYtCFND6eQ9cSbBfWGcDNyusXX8NxSwt7WwiruGtHv2ppcDoNvezZ4vXZ38kADFXFxSrcE8oWM9p-BHeoyXwVVXOwtiVOqrWBWeHnB8yb6Gs2J5Q.qTORKppslJpVb1GrQ9H9NJQlKKh3FsQQ7kMAeyLlOCg&dib_tag=se&keywords=Hilda+Van+Stockum&qid=1708994964&s=audible&sr=1-4)_, and _[Friendly Gables](https://www.amazon.com/Friendly-Gables-Hilda-Van-Stockum-audiobook/dp/B01MFCUSM8/ref=sr_1_6?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.lSltNmEfnS0tcen9H3hwSVoFID3vr_IQNoVmgbqYtCFND6eQ9cSbBfWGcDNyusXX8NxSwt7WwiruGtHv2ppcDoNvezZ4vXZ38kADFXFxSrcE8oWM9p-BHeoyXwVVXOwtiVOqrWBWeHnB8yb6Gs2J5Q.qTORKppslJpVb1GrQ9H9NJQlKKh3FsQQ7kMAeyLlOCg&dib_tag=se&keywords=Hilda+Van+Stockum&qid=1708994964&s=audible&sr=1-6)_. The reader follows five siblings on the American homefront during World War II as they become young adults. Humor, innocence, and a focus on [sibling bonds](/2023/10/lasting-sibling-relationships/) pervade Van Stockum’s stories.

3. _[Men of Iron](https://www.amazon.com/Men-of-Iron-Howard-Pyle-audiobook/dp/B0002P0CZQ/ref=sr_1_2?crid=35GK0HO7TMEQD&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.LBKHvi-UjTBjqcY0PZVcHNCjgBIi5eKZs4VDpPPYd4Bsjvql-ybWBlKbD1ZMvIK8zPLd-bPT4rHvbBxzpLNnhxyZLNTPj5Ggss0ZF29rR4xWw6LhKHM7SwT6UuAid8tgrnmo5tZ4lUIOr7UM1nzhmQFXM2tQYpdsaN9Uc7h4CdfNvY0AWRoJiBdsJL2lyCxtu7D5h6-h6cNLQsSaUQmvCaNfUsOYVVQ00HZUl7T6Bw0.GiUwah6AfWngFNxfI2ii6fUIHkFQYFaXuQepvQ9D4fM&dib_tag=se&keywords=men+Of+iron&qid=1708995261&s=audible&sprefix=men+of+iro%2Caudible%2C270&sr=1-2)_ by Howard Pyle (10+). Known for his fairytale prose and classical Western mythology themes, Pyle penned (and illustrated) a coming-of-age novel that follows a medieval squire’s journey to becoming a knight. The book also features historical information about weaponry, chivalry, and knighthood ceremonies.

4. _[Understood Betsy](https://www.amazon.com/Understood-Betsy-audiobook/dp/B018Y2C4TA/ref=sr_1_1?crid=1FQZ8MEEKTZ7L&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.3fZ97t-squqT6xVHE9LRG1H_epliBEwa7KWo8aUEDdoQ1cl1xHjj4ZsXqgHPLwXTmWIPcQ9WmVJIFwfHlPXrelsTNEKBuoaQCY_0H0HPrFE.vTQVOaY-FcYqfFORxAt2QG1jjYDycrSQKgqiiUqIkAU&dib_tag=se&keywords=understood+betsy&qid=1708995493&s=audible&sprefix=understood+bets%2Caudible%2C271&sr=1-1)_ by Dorothy Canfield Fisher (10+). This follows an endearing young heroine as she goes from being a completely dependent orphan to being an independent and capable farm girl. Unsurprisingly, Fisher was a believer in the then-unknown Maria Montessori’s educational theories.

5. _Dragons in Our Midst_ series by Bryan Davis (12+). _[Raising Dragons](https://www.amazon.com/Raising-Dragons-Bryan-Davis-audiobook/dp/B001YJC01I/ref=sr_1_1?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.a9hZar2BLKpLIYvT3iAkAdfSTNpCakDh18H_gw_pGiePI224897ZFsgz-vggdkOS7L3D5FNfNamsdPoz7Ew9XnGWmA8WvQlBweKKmbgpggSPMlsaKwapdCE4PwcugJKm.SSqZb8cK5to0D8jQKkiIZwyonMmuO6AwYsfjDbq4jQA&dib_tag=se&keywords=dragons+in+our+midst&qid=1708995788&s=audible&sr=1-1)_, _[The Candlestone](https://www.amazon.com/The-Candlestone-Bryan-Davis-audiobook/dp/B001L4EEMY/ref=sr_1_2?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.a9hZar2BLKpLIYvT3iAkAdfSTNpCakDh18H_gw_pGiePI224897ZFsgz-vggdkOS7L3D5FNfNamsdPoz7Ew9XnGWmA8WvQlBweKKmbgpggSPMlsaKwapdCE4PwcugJKm.SSqZb8cK5to0D8jQKkiIZwyonMmuO6AwYsfjDbq4jQA&dib_tag=se&keywords=dragons+in+our+midst&qid=1708995830&s=audible&sr=1-2)_, _[Circles of Seven](https://www.amazon.com/Circles-of-Seven-Bryan-Davis-audiobook/dp/B001L4EEMO/ref=sr_1_7?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.RE9ceO9yJ7Er1A978FiivDtC2Q_lphgkgdxj99NZQHsr7jPUX3_Pv8wpQvZktKjc-IWtFE4fC2_qrf-C5msjrFMUJCrcX8tIIhYIy-lp8_Qc83Dgz9oPb6g1wnMP4Fhea9QAz6Ha6gOIfl0a-QX5TdlUy40UHeQ-knRfCvK3Gt469xqTE-w8Tq2YImZ6BXd-FoAxByLPFZMjSQZ0LHgSDOWHKUa3DqgzpTz6Kz1YfPg.dIdBX2eO0UkqCTC4PbQxrigl36dMEHKzZeQEGLNLTIo&dib_tag=se&keywords=Bryan+Davis&qid=1708995903&s=audible&sr=1-7)_, and _[Tears of a Dragon](https://www.amazon.com/Tears-of-Dragon-Bryan-Davis-audiobook/dp/B001HOAEXQ/ref=sr_1_10?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.RE9ceO9yJ7Er1A978FiivDtC2Q_lphgkgdxj99NZQHsr7jPUX3_Pv8wpQvZktKjc-IWtFE4fC2_qrf-C5msjrFMUJCrcX8tIIhYIy-lp8_Qc83Dgz9oPb6g1wnMP4Fhea9QAz6Ha6gOIfl0a-QX5TdlUy40UHeQ-knRfCvK3Gt469xqTE-w8Tq2YImZ6BXd-FoAxByLPFZMjSQZ0LHgSDOWHKUa3DqgzpTz6Kz1YfPg.dIdBX2eO0UkqCTC4PbQxrigl36dMEHKzZeQEGLNLTIo&dib_tag=se&keywords=Bryan+Davis&qid=1708995903&s=audible&sr=1-10)_ follow two young teens who discover they are not fully human but are actually half dragon—which, of course, entails embarking on dangerous quests. Davis manages to instill the popular fantasy genre with a blend of traditional values.

6. _[The Golden Name Day](https://www.amazon.com/Golden-Name-Day-Age-Library/dp/1948959305/ref=sr_1_1?crid=369MFRY0GO4W8&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.y-7-drFPA9Lru_1hwdDtSShwwmQuZV65iJxBoXNAvDD9Ds2SHhMYF7sD5t_hTKTVLNctIiLEQcV5l2sNCIzhliTeQE3ZjGALna3lANrHPTVC9bswDy-7tm8GyHMkVRyl8vXljeGkzSLxyAm9bDFWnuvQkITBOnglLixaEABbCgv0K4Xx8Bdm7SnPBXAJ_5Z3jpf5rgC9uHV-f4iLnH_RwwsP6nYKYnP81eeXKMrqf6O3ehu9U21LbdN8gwkIBUAFrA5G_Fo8LTF-OGoPObGGN-_eRtulN268OOzw87srxkA.J1La03jFo9CQ27qCEtIWrwzoDiMpeikcqoL1oSaJJxY&dib_tag=se&keywords=the+golden+name+day&qid=1708996950&sprefix=the+golden+nameday%2Caps%2C534&sr=8-1)_ by Jennie D. Lindquist (8+). This book is based on the traditional Swedish celebration of birthdays and follows a 9-year-old girl who goes to live on her grandparents’ farm. There is one sequel titled _[The Little Silver House](https://www.amazon.com/little-silver-house-Jennie-Lindquist/dp/B0007DKIP2/ref=sr_1_2?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.Z22eE0WwlHGN4pjFI3gjXyyu5QleAydm38zyqRQEFmEKQKEvFBJeSsLH7EVywASB.qTn4ddT3usVqSGkf2dyLTDQC6CzOKVcJ03IemtZzGp0&dib_tag=se&qid=1708996972&refinements=p_27%3AJennie+D+Lindquist&s=books&sr=1-2&text=Jennie+D+Lindquist)_.

7. _[Shane](https://www.amazon.com/Shane-Jack-Schaefer-ebook/dp/B0725P6KR2/ref=sr_1_1?crid=1EMQ90VUJF4J&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.2brpXYpe3bQkMYSGRAR0b9cQm3uwxxq37xS_oAQEEg0_kIQb3tUWkWKFO-jK8TX_dktaGjIUXpugiH9-RffiY9Vh_yKcLuEXWAtkMZJq6pHqu-LjTiebZbQ5LIG2mMUqvffwktCp2QwvRqe5rnnC9GBWaI0-Qt3Q1wfbrsnbxXwZgY0VDlFpvQH-4eO3gAKE5CoBdA_jGrEO-GookF52TAa1RGArbGOxQXjGgmfilQ4.BIvIu_WzdUxfxB_nDfSx73pVcHdvF3k9yjeBZHquDXM&dib_tag=se&keywords=shane+by&qid=1708997098&s=books&sprefix=shane+by+%2Cstripbooks%2C249&sr=1-1)_ by Jack Schaefer (12+). This is a classic Western novel for boys that follows a mysterious gunslinger as he joins forces with Wyoming pioneers to save their homes from a crooked cattle driver. Schaefer includes themes of virtue and man’s search for self.

8. _[The Penderwicks](https://www.amazon.com/Penderwicks-Paperback-5-Book-Boxed-Set/dp/0593378105/ref=sr_1_1?crid=3LWJ3I16ZQLQ0&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.cAvX-rzZe9UWsVTI0-wpjLDlUM5aunjyqkV7O7eyNEIBtiQbbHB5yIywuwV3ELgM4XhVxctrWw9tsRkXI9xvQugfBi_DdHQODDTt3nxI-_VRCr5ovSoYDTtk5Ly6zv9YcVRAZKUYQ_0EeACrClA9nCLlweaqhmqvhkUc-K8r0oaQLTUKkmGK1jaJJ_nqfMAuvUK3wtYoIvJo8G1VnR2eKt_3A4g4h0DL79uTqAXZqSQ.GZHrMPLQfEiiILluGwDmhboUY-3g2sIbpDFjp6aFIRk&dib_tag=se&keywords=the+penderwicks+series&qid=1708997284&s=books&sprefix=the+penderwicks+seri%2Cstripbooks%2C245&sr=1-1)_ by Jeanne Birdsall (10+). This five-volume series follows five sisters from childhood to adulthood, along with their friends, relatives, and devoted father. Described as a modern classic, the book has themes of humor, childhood innocence, adventure, and the true meaning of family.

9. _[Impossible](https://www.amazon.com/Impossible-Scarborough-Fair-Trilogy-Book-ebook/dp/B08P9TFDQR/ref=sr_1_1?crid=16WM3Z8ZXQMYR&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.6c9zKJRiubX0tQV0mUCPfJ8Npq9OOPl50Eczelo3PLnGjHj071QN20LucGBJIEps.JFUMGbA5BzP25NS7kgUkspH8coF_XTU1rujy--rIy0k&dib_tag=se&keywords=impossible+werlin&qid=1708997618&s=books&sprefix=impossible+werli%2Cstripbooks%2C237&sr=1-1)_ by Nancy Werlin (16+). This is a modern retelling of the classic fairytale curse. WARNING: The story includes a nongraphic rape scene, so please read it yourself first to determine whether you wish to suggest it to your children. Surprisingly, the book supports the triumph of traditional values, such as life beginning at conception, young marriage, and love as a choice. There are two sequels, but they do not feature traditional values.

Book one of the _Chronicles of Arundel_, this is a colonial American adventure that follows a young soldier who joins forces with Benedict Arnold’s quest for Quebec. Known as a history book first and fictional adventure second, the book is heavy on geographical beauty, character development, and patriotism. (There are three more books in the series as well.)

11. _[Redwall](https://www.amazon.com/Redwall-Brian-Jacques-audiobook/dp/B0000X8R8E/ref=sr_1_1?crid=3A2BNJKTHHZ6T&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.Ody3CvSCl4lVCDhkbC4KagIzJ2OEdvS_13yztfWowopZUs472ATzMca9TkQHj2sx_JZnMB2-BYNX0_BlYKjcAaRrs2A0Qi1MxnUR3ItYjtckDrvGcDXOJOZlHocHbGEIHG957_Q-3dftibL4fRqzjUgjSjKGgowySNofWXC6LF6NYrXOnE7tD2uNBMvmmAG1Pxblb6DifnMm4ssmem2f63XLau6AYm4XDyJl7Zs3scU.7nUHYJGNbNhxVmi7OHMi-jYUTv06RY4yJUju8KKHhew&dib_tag=se&keywords=the+redwall+series&qid=1708998170&s=books&sprefix=the+redwall+seri%2Cstripbooks%2C273&sr=1-1)_ by Brian Jacques (10+). This is the first of 22 novels that follow the intertwining adventures of a colorful cast of small mammals. Hares, mice, badgers, rats, snakes, otters, and more all feature in the fictional land of Mossflower. Recurring themes throughout the books include traditional values like truth, courage, valor, justice, and honor. (Other personal favorites in the series include _Marlfox, Taggerung, Rakkety Tam, Doomwyte_, and _The Long Patrol_.)

This story follows the true adventures of a Canadian boy who raises all kinds of wild animals in his home. Mowat also has another book for slightly older readers called _[The Dog Who Wouldn’t Be](https://www.amazon.com/Dog-Who-Wouldnt-Be/dp/1567926126/?_encoding=UTF8&pd_rd_w=8Zixm&content-id=amzn1.sym.cf86ec3a-68a6-43e9-8115-04171136930a&pf_rd_p=cf86ec3a-68a6-43e9-8115-04171136930a&pf_rd_r=132-8718332-3366319&pd_rd_wg=b9pXC&pd_rd_r=aecd09a7-4fb0-41f5-8afe-e06460df0a77&ref_=aufs_ap_sc_dsk)_.

13. _[Little Britches: Father and I Were Ranchers](https://www.amazon.com/Father-and-I-Were-Ranchers-audiobook/dp/B001JHT7DE/ref=sr_1_1?crid=2QNW81N3FFV31&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.nBYb83FY8a6Bd0vo4TwYfPppTWzW9fvg_VekpNRhHTl15L69BbrtxYhC1CHgEviLHcxQ4UqMwlesyTUi36Tn9QOaKszQ31jeGVYC_y6aFerbJQLjZ4d-ERla7FKRzURoXeIn_C0WYMqShWN_2fZz4h59goOCJKk9-cBoFHW7F0_mczNUWfVlOorf46QmyyQMbw7AVZqQnztIlo4pIv8muPGLhb3POEz3TeZQvWDuaj4.DHg0W7DtZGbeP_Y26VP5RoO7dQeSaXzDLhoIpi6bJSo&dib_tag=se&keywords=little+britches+series&qid=1708998557&s=books&sprefix=little+britches+seri%2Cstripbooks%2C253&sr=1-1)_ by Ralph Moody (10+). This series of eight books is like a boy’s version of the Laura Ingalls Wilder series. Follow Ralph as a young boy from the time his family moves to the Wild West at the turn of the 20th century all the way to his growing up to become a horse stuntman in Hollywood. (Spoiler for sensitive readers: His beloved father dies at the end of the first book.)

14. _[Flower Fairies Treasury](https://www.amazon.com/Flower-Fairies-Treasury-Cicely-BARKER/dp/072324409X/ref=sr_1_2?crid=QO2AABGL4D5C&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.wf09oB7MtcTUgflx2Euem0DIiJWTgRMuqCFdrDCRgtQQVjW4XaODlXoyD4WhevU8kP_IWzL1lhLe9Q3gX9sKttamgRJSiAWwZexH0-YzyWK1Zz5DpKktUf5tdFucmd1dcCO-PRRuI3tWHn2o5T4NPR_NEysQ0_UFbVizYYb05meVVMK5Lr_2KNNyHPC20RZYIO67uVeNUNqtc4gQ3EjsNnyxL-1HWwoNfzGpGTrwzeI.laTuq1c3PttdAH8Z5PEOAkvxcKeWrosM9AjZPXP35MU&dib_tag=se&keywords=cicely+mary+barker+treasury&qid=1708998649&s=audible&sprefix=cicely+mary+barker+treasur%2Caudible%2C238&sr=1-2)_ by Cicely Mary Barker (8+). This beloved poet and illustrator created a vast portfolio over the early decades of the 20th century. Her watercolor paintings are a celebration of nature and make a fantastic introduction to classical verse for young readers.
