As the most widely observed cultural holiday in the world, Christmas produces many things—joy, happiness, gratitude, reverence. And numbers. Lots of peculiar, often large, numbers. Here are a few to contemplate this season:

[$75](http://www.statista.com/statistics/209238/mean-average-dollars-spent-on-rea-and-fake-christmas-trees-in-the-us/) – Average amount U.S. consumers spent on real Christmas trees in 2017.

[$107](https://www.statista.com/statistics/209238/mean-average-dollars-spent-on-rea-and-fake-christmas-trees-in-the-us/) – Average amount U.S. consumers spent on fake Christmas trees in 2017.

[27,400,000](https://www.statista.com/statistics/209249/purchase-figures-for-real-and-fake-christmas-trees-in-the-us/) – Number of real Christmas trees sold in the U.S. in 2017.

[21,100,000](https://www.statista.com/statistics/209249/purchase-figures-for-real-and-fake-christmas-trees-in-the-us/) – Number of fake Christmas trees sold in the U.S. in 2017.

[7](http://www.statisticbrain.com/christmas-tree-statistics/) – Average growing time in years for a Christmas tree.

[350 million](http://www.statisticbrain.com/christmas-tree-statistics/) – Number of Christmas trees currently growing on Christmas tree farms.

[329.2 million](http://www.census.gov/popclock/) – Current population of the United States.

[$27.21](http://www.statista.com/statistics/212196/christmas-lights-energy-costs-in-the-us/) — The energy costs of lighting a six-foot Christmas tree, lit 12 hours a day for 40 days, decorated with various light types.

[$1,500,000,000](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/facts-for-features/2017/holiday.html) – Estimated value of U.S. imports of Christmas tree ornaments from China between January and September 2016.

[$22,700,000,000](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/facts-for-features/2017/holiday.html) – Estimated retail sales by the nation’s department stores (including leased departments) in December 2016. This represents a decrease of $1 billion in retail sales from December of the previous year.

[42.7 percent](http://nccs.urban.org/data-statistics/charitable-giving-america-some-facts-and-figures) — Estimated percentage of charitable giving that occurs between Thanksgiving and New Year’s.

[$794](https://www.statista.com/statistics/246963/christmas-spending-in-the-us-during-november/) – Average amount people in the U.S. estimated they’ll spend on Christmas presents in 2018.

[108,000,000](http://www.baltimoremd.com/humor/santaengineer.html) — Average number of homes Santa Claus has to visit on December 25 (assuming there is at least one “nice” child in each).
