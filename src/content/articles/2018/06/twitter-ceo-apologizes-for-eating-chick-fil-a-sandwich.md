Twitter CEO Jack Dorsey on Sunday expressed regret over eating at Chick-fil-A because of the chicken company’s CEO’s personal views on gay marriage.

Dorsey tweeted a screenshot from his phone that showed a purchase he had made at Chick-fil-A using a mobile application. After a liberal backlash, however, Dorsey apologized for eating at the popular fast-food restaurant.

At issue was Chick-fil-A CEO Dan Cathy’s 2012 [support](https://www.washingtonpost.com/blogs/post-leadership/post/chick-fil-a-president-dan-cathy-bites-into-gay-marriage-debate/2012/07/19/gJQACrvzvW_blog.html?noredirect=on&utm_term=.8d72df7f062e) for defining marriage as between a man and a woman, which he described as “the biblical definition of a family.”

Former CNN anchor Soledad O’Brien was among those to call out Dorsey for eating at the self-described [home of the original chicken sandwich](https://www.chick-fil-a.com/).

O’Brien indicated that Dorsey had sinned by publicly eating at Chick-fil-A during gay pride month.

_(Screenshot/Twitter)_

“You’re right,” Dorsey conceded. “Completely forgot about their background.”
