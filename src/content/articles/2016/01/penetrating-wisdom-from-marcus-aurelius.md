**1\. “Waste no more time arguing what a good man should be. Be one.”**

**2\. “Never esteem anything as of advantage to you that will make you break your word or lose your self-respect.”**

**3\. “Nowhere can man find a quieter or more untroubled retreat than in his own soul.”**

**4\. “There is a limit to the time assigned you, and if you don’t use it to free yourself it will be gone and never return.”**

**5\. “Adapt yourself to the environment in which your lot has been cast, and show true love to the fellow-mortals with whom destiny has surrounded you.”**

**6\. “When you wake up in the morning, tell yourself: The people I deal with today will be meddling, ungrateful, arrogant, dishonest, jealous, and surly. They are like this because they can’t tell good from evil.”**

**7\. “You suffer justly if you choose to become good tomorrow rather than to be good today.”**

**8\. “Our life is what our thoughts make it.”**
