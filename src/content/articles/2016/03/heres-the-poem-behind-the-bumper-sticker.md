If you drive around a bit, you’ve probably seen a “Not all who Wander are Lost” bumper sticker. The phrase actually comes from this lovely poem: 

_All that is gold does not glitter,_  
_Not all those who wander are lost;_  
_The old that is strong does not wither,_  
_Deep roots are not reached by the frost._

_From the ashes a fire shall be woken,_  
_A light from the shadows shall spring;_  
_Renewed shall be blade that was broken,_  
_The crownless again shall be king._

The author of the poem is J.R.R. Tolkien who included it twice in _The Fellowship of the Ring_, part of the _Lord of the Rings_ trilogy. The poem itself is a reference to Aragorn and his wandering until he returns as the king of Gondor. ?

Of course, you probably knew all of that already. But if you didn’t, now you do.
