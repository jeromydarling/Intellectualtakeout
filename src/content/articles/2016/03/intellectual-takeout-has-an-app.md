It’s been too long in coming, but Intellectual Takeout now has an app. While it’s live on Apple’s App Store, it’s still just a beta version. You can download it for your iPhone by clicking the link below:

[Intellectual Takeout App](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/intellectual-takeout/id1078028666?mt=8)

Let us know what you think and what you’d like to see on it. We have some ideas that we’re working on, but we want to hear from you!

![](/sites/ito/files/ito_app.png)

If you’re an Android user, don’t worry. We’ll be rolling out an app for Android-based devices as soon as possible.

And, again, let us know what you’d like to see in an Intellectual Takeout app since this is just a beta version. You can message us on Facebook or shoot us an e-mail at [contact@intellectualtakeout.org](mailto:contact@intellectualtakeout.org). Thanks!
