We share with you a list of the ten smartest celebrities [WatchMojo](http://watchmojo.com/) could find and obtain video footage of without violating copyright laws.

Now, a list of the 10 smartest celebrities might sound like a fairly low bar, but I’ll give credit where credit is due. Most of the people on this list are pretty impressive. (Note to Alex Trebek: Let’s get some of _these_ people on Celebrity Jeopardy.)

Without further ado:

**10) Natalie Portman**

**![](/sites/ito/files/large_natalie-portman.jpg)**

The Oscar-winning actress has a degree in psychology from Harvard, which doesn’t exactly blow me away. The whole Hebrew-speaking thing is rather impressive, though. But I’m docking her points for that atrocious music video (see video below).

**9) Conan O’Brien**

![](/sites/ito/files/large_conan_0.jpg)

Another Harvard alum, Conan studied history and literature at the Ivy League school and was valedictorian of his high school. Good enough for me.

8) **Jodie Foster**

![](/sites/ito/files/large_jodie-foster-taxi-driver.jpg)

The Oscar-winner rose to fame as a child star on the silver screen, but people often forget that Foster was a bit of a prodigy as a youngster. She was reading at age 3, can speak several European languages, and graduated _cum laude_ from Yale.

**7) Lisa Kudrow**

![](/sites/ito/files/large_phoebe.jpg)

Kudrow is famous for playing the endearing but simple Phoebe Buffay on _Friends_. Unlike the “Smelly Cat” singer, Kudrow reportedly has a “highly academic history.” The clip doesn’t exactly explain what that is, but we’re told she worked on her father’s medical staff and became an expert in headaches. Oh, and her brother is a neurologist. So there.

**6) Rowan Atkinson**

![](https://chroniclesmagazine-legacy.ae-admin.com/assets/3/28/large_mr._bean_0.jpg)

I didn’t actually know who this was, and I’m guessing some of you don’t either. He’s Mr. Bean, and Mr. Bean apparently has a Master’s Degree in electrical engineering from Oxford. Who knew?

**5) James Woods**

**![](/sites/ito/files/large_james_woods_casino.jpg)**

Woods has kind of disappeared from Hollywood of late, perhaps because of his [outspoken](https://twitter.com/RealJamesWoods) political views. Fortunately, the _Casino_ star has a brain to fall back on. He reportedly scored a perfect 800 on the verbal portion of the SAT, studied at MIT, and is said to have an IQ around 180 (which places him in the top third or so of Intellectual Takeout readership).

**4) Mayim Bialik**

**![](/sites/ito/files/large_mayim.jpg)**

Bialik plays Sheldon Cooper’s love interest neurobiologist on the hit CBS comedy _The Big Bang Theory_. But in real-life she has a doctorate degree in neuroscience. Not too shabby.

**3) John Cleese**

![](/sites/ito/files/large_cleese.jpg)

The Monty Python star is an established comic genius, but he apparently boasts some impressive academic credentials as well. A graduate of Cambridge, Cleese holds a degree in law. He also speaks with an English accent, which makes him sound really smart.  

**2) Dolph Lundgren**

**![](/sites/ito/files/large_drago.jpg)**

The man who portrayed Ivan “I Must Break You” Drago holds several degrees in chemistry and chemical engineering from various academic institutions around the world. And he’s got [a huge Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/DolphLundgrenOfficial/?fref=ts), so somebody should share this article on Facebook and tag him so he shares it. We love you, Dolph!

**1) Brian May**

**![](/sites/ito/files/large_brian_may.jpg)**

May, who rose to fame as the lead guitarist of Queen, earned a doctorate in astrophysics from Imperial College London in 2007. He has since authored several books with titles like _A Survey of Radical Velocities in the Zodiacal Dust Cloud_. Weighty stuff.

**Honorable Mentions:** Ken Jeong, Ben Stein, Cindy Crawford, Geena Davis, Jon Stewart
