**1\. “It is only with the heart that one can see rightly; what is essential is invisible to the eye.”**

**2\. “The machine does not isolate us from the great problems of nature but plunges us more deeply into them.”**

**3\. “Life has taught us that love does not consist in gazing at each other but in looking outward together in the same direction.”**

**4\. “To be a man is to be _responsible_: to be ashamed of miseries you did not cause; to be proud of your comrades’ victories; to be aware, when setting one stone, that you are building a world.”**

**5\. “‘What makes the desert beautiful,’ said the little prince, ‘is that somewhere it hides a well.’”**

**6\. “Your task is not to foresee the future, but to enable it.”**

**7\. “You become responsible, forever, for what you have tamed.”**

**8\. “A civilization is built on what is required of men, not on that which is provided for them.”**
