Things are clearly getting heated in politics with the arrival of Donald Trump. Since the Chicago, anti-Trump protests broke out in violence, the subsequent anti-Trump protests seem to be escalating in violence whether in New Mexico, San Diego, or San Jose.

The most recent videos are filled with physical assaults against pro-Trump individuals, destruction of property, pro-socialism signs, Mexican flags, and burning American flags. Here’s the coverage of the most recent violent protesters:

Disagreeing with Trump and his supporters is a perfectly reasonable thing to do. But this? Is this the kind of behavior that is supposed to win people to the anti-Trump side?
