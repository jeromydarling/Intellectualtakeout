[Download Intellectual Takeout’s App 2.0!](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/intellectualtakeout.org/id1105079715?mt=8)

 The changes made the Intellectual Takeout app really awesome. Even more awesome is the news that Apple has approved version 2.0 and it’s available for downloading. Below are some screenshots of what you should expect.
