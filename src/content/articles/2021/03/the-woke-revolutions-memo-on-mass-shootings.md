_Memo in light of recent events, for the immediate attention of all political and media leaders aligned with the Woke Revolution, which is to say, just about all of them:_

Mass shootings like those that just took place in Atlanta and Boulder are a tragedy, of course. But they are also one of the best propaganda tools for the contemporary Woke Movement to advance the agenda, if framed and discussed properly. As you know, the Woke Revolution [believes that no crisis](https://www.breitbart.com/politics/2020/05/27/nancy-pelosi-every-crisis-including-coronavirus-is-an-opportunity-to-advance-leftist-agenda/) (i.e. opportunity for propagandizing) should ever pass without harnessing it to our agenda. The following guidelines, produced by experts in Woke Thought and Activism, delineate the best way to talk about mass shootings:

*   When information on specifics is limited, the best practice is to blame easy access to guns, pledging to curtail that access. Use the opportunity to [blast the National Rifle Association](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/us/days-before-boulder-shooting-nra-celebrated-defeat-of-city-s-assault-weapons-ban/ar-BB1eSRRI?ocid=uxbndlbing) and other groups that advocate for Second Amendment rights.

*   Give [editorial space to ideologues](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2021/03/23/senate-hearing-2-gun-bills-follows-boulder-atlanta-mass-shootings/6962816002/) masquerading as scholars who can frame the American constitutional right to gun ownership as a “public health crisis.”

*   Ignore inconvenient facts about gun ownership and violence in the U.S. The fact that mass shootings [make up a very tiny fraction](https://www.washingtonpost.com/graphics/2018/national/mass-shootings-in-america/) of the murders in this country every year does not advance the Woke Agenda, nor do the facts that [the vast majority of crimes committed while in possession of a firearm were done so with illegally acquired guns](https://patriotpost.us/articles/60599-90-percent-of-guns-used-in-crime-obtained-illegally-2019-01-17), and that [legally owned guns](https://fee.org/articles/guns-prevent-thousands-of-crimes-every-day-research-show/) play a role in preventing a vast amount of crime every year.

**SUMMARY**: When in doubt, [it’s always the guns](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/unfathomable-pain-democrats-demand-action-gun-control-after-back-back-n1261797). The Woke Revolution believes there are too many guns (in the hands of citizens, that is—[members of the cultural elite will of course need armed guards to keep themselves safe](https://www.breitbart.com/politics/2020/03/02/watch-bloomberg-tries-to-defend-using-armed-guards-while-pushing-gun-control/)) and citizen disarmament is a righteous and undisputable goal.
