One of China’s main [propaganda](https://www.dailysignal.com/2020/04/03/investigating-chinas-coronavirus-propaganda-wildfire/) outlets has paid American newspapers nearly $19 million for advertising and printing expenses over the past four years, according to documents filed with the Justice Department.

_China Daily_, an English-language newspaper controlled by the Chinese Communist Party, has paid more than $4.6 million to _The Washington Post_ and nearly $6 million to _The Wall Street Journal_ since November 2016, the [records](https://efile.fara.gov/docs/3457-Amendment-20200601-2.pdf) show.

Both newspapers have published [paid supplements](https://dailycaller.com/2017/11/29/china-paid-wapo-to-publish-a-news-supplement-that-ignored-north-koreas-icbm/) that _China Daily_ produces called “China Watch.” The inserts are designed to look like real news articles, though they often contain a pro-Beijing spin on contemporary news events.

[One insert](https://twitter.com/hunterschwarz/status/1045004554847342598) from September 2018 touted an initiative pushed by Chinese President Xi Jinping with the headline: “Belt and Road aligns with African nations.” The same insert ran a story titled “Tariffs to take toll on U.S. homebuyers” that asserted that U.S. tariffs on Chinese lumber would raise the cost of building homes in the United States.

_China Daily_ also paid for advertising in several other newspapers: _The New York Times_ ($50,000), _Foreign Policy_ ($240,000), _The Des Moines Register_ ($34,600), and _CQ-Roll Call_ ($76,000).

It spent a total of $11,002,628 on advertising in U.S. newspapers, and another $265,822 on advertising with Twitter.

_China Daily_ has also paid out more than $7.6 million to newspapers and printing companies for its newspaper for U.S.-based readers, the Justice Department’s filings show.

_The Los Angeles Times_, _The Seattle Times_, _The Atlanta Journal-Constitution_, the _Chicago Tribune_, the _Houston Chronicle_, and _The Boston Globe_ are all listed as clients of _China Daily_. The Chinese outlet paid the _Los Angeles Times_ $657,523 for printing services, according to the Foreign Agents Registration Act filings.

The Justice Department has for years required _China Daily_ to disclose its activities semi-annually under the Foreign Agents Registration Act. The most recent filing, which _China Daily_ submitted on June 1, is the first to include detailed breakdowns of payments to American news outlets. The outlet disclosed those expenditures for the period between November 2016 and April 2020.

It is unclear if China Daily submitted the more detailed filings under pressure from the Justice Department or on its own.

Pro-democracy groups [have long warned](https://dailycaller.com/2020/01/15/washington-post-nyt-china-watch-propaganda-freedom-house/) about the Chinese government’s attempts to push propaganda through American news outlets. [Freedom House](https://freedomhouse.org/report/special-report/2020/beijings-global-megaphone) and the [Hoover Institution](https://www.hoover.org/sites/default/files/research/docs/chineseinfluence_americaninterests_fullreport_web.pdf) have both drawn attention to _China Daily_’s paid inserts in reports on the Chinese government’s efforts to influence the [media](https://www.dailysignal.com/2020/04/01/liberal-medias-double-standard-censor-trumps-briefings-air-chinas-propaganda/).

_China Daily_ and other Beijing-controlled propaganda mills have come under intense scrutiny amid the [coronavirus pandemic](https://www.dailysignal.com/2020/06/03/what-do-we-know-about-status-of-pandemic-heres-some-data-to-put-it-in-perspective/). Chinese government officials have tried to [divert blame](https://www.dailysignal.com/2020/03/12/chinese-official-suggests-us-introduced-coronavirus-to-wuhan-says-us-owes-us-an-explanation/) for the [spread](https://www.dailysignal.com/2020/03/11/amid-chinas-denials-national-security-adviser-blames-communist-regime-for-swift-spread-of-coronavirus/) of the virus to the United States and other Western nations. Many of the regime-controlled outlets, including _China Daily_, have echoed the communist leaders’ talking points.

_China Daily_ has slowed its advertising expenditures in recent months, the filings show. _China Daily_ last paid _The Washington Post_ for advertising in December 2019. Its payments to _The Wall Street Journal_ have been less than half their average amount since February 2020, the documents show.

_China Daily_ did not respond to a request for comment.
