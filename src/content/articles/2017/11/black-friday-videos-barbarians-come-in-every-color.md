These days it’s popular to view whites and their history as particularly barbaric. We’re told to look at the wars, look at the colonization, look at the slavery. But those making those remarks seem to have forgotten that all of those things have taken place in nearly every civilization and by every race throughout history.

As much good as there is in us, there also lurks a savage side. No race is immune to the intoxicating effects of the mob. Black Friday is a great example. In the videos below, behold people of seemingly every race and ethnicity acting like utter savages — and that’s just to get a good deal! Imagine what we can do to each other with the right (really, wrong) political rhetoric.
