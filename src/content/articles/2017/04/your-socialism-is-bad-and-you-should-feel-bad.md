I’m tempted to say that statism is sort of like a cult. Proponents of [socialism](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2010/04/23/obama-is-not-a-socialist/) and [other big-government ideologies](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2014/03/11/whether-you-call-it-socialism-statism-fascism-or-corporatism-big-government-is-evil-and-destructive/) have a dogmatic zeal that blinds them to reality.

For instance, no nation has [ever become rich with big government](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2014/09/24/a-two-question-challenge-for-supporters-of-intervention-and-big-government/). But that doesn’t stop leftists from advocating in favor of higher taxes and more coercive redistribution.

![](http://fee.org/media/22180/bfcf8-582039_445785908786586_478714167_n.jpg?width=359&height=276)They are equally capable of rationalizing that economic misery in places such as [Greece](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2016/11/02/the-greek-crisis-caused-by-statism-exacerbated-by-statism/) and [Venezuela](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2016/12/11/venezuela-and-santa-claus-vs-thomas-sowell-and-the-little-red-hen/) has nothing to do with bad policy, and you can even find a few zealots willing to defend basket cases such as [Cuba](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2016/09/04/the-economic-misery-of-cuban-communism/) and [North Korea](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2011/12/19/the-brutal-impact-of-north-korean-statism/).

So long as they don’t burn me at the stake for my heretical views, I guess I won’t get too agitated by their bizarre fetish for statism.

But I will periodically mock them. And that’s the purpose of today’s column. We’ll start with this nice comparison between a capitalist grocery store and a socialist grocery store. I have no idea, by the way, if the lower image actually is a supermarket in a socialist country, but let’s not forget that [a real-world version of this comparison](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2015/10/04/the-texas-grocery-store-that-helped-push-the-evil-empire-on-to-the-ash-heap-of-history/) is one of the reasons there’s no longer an Evil Empire.

![](http://fee.org/media/22179/v00dntf.jpg?width=600&height=600)

But the bad news about socialism is not limited to economic deprivation for the masses.

The system also leads in many cases to totalitarianism (see [this article](https://reason.com/archives/2017/04/04/from-socialism-to-dictatorship) by Marian Tupy, for example).

Venezuela is a particularly poignant example. Once the richest nation in Latin America, it now is [an economic laggard](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2011/03/28/in-one-chart-everything-you-wanted-to-know-about-the-relationship-between-good-policy-and-economic-prosperity/) and also is [a cesspool of oppression](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2016/07/30/venezuela-is-heading-for-the-fourth-circle-of-statist-hell/).

Which makes [this set of images](https://www.reddit.com/r/Libertarian/comments/64fcxi/what_happens_when_socialists_disagree_with_you/) from _Reddit_‘s libertarian page both funny and sad.

![](http://fee.org/media/22178/d85v5idyclqy.png?width=600&height=224.48630136986304)

As you might expect, Milton Friedman had some very pointed observations on this topic.

The really good part starts shortly before 2:00. He explains very clearly that socialism is [based on force and coercion](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2014/12/08/government-coercion-is-the-common-link-between-nazism-communism-and-other-forms-of-socialism/).

I’ve saved the best for last.

The [PotL](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2013/12/25/a-golden-rule-christmas/) sent me this collection of risky temptations and it perfectly captures the attitude of many statists. No matter how many times socialism has failed, they never learn [the appropriate lesson](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2016/04/10/capitalism-rules-socialism-drools/). It just hasn’t been tried by the right people, they tell us. Or been imposed in the right circumstances.

So they want us to give it one more try, just like a person with no willpower will eat one more bite of chocolate.

![](http://fee.org/media/22177/2017-04-09-photo-00002385.jpg?width=600&height=554.2857142857143)

Which is the same message you find [here](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2016/03/06/socialism-evil-and-stupid/), [here](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2013/12/28/the-biggest-risk-factor-leading-to-premature-death-is/), and [here](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2013/12/16/some-unusual-obama-humor/).

Incidentally, this analysis not only applies to socialism, as [technically defined](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2012/06/12/obamas-technically-not-a-socialist-but-he-wont-like-how-thomas-sowell-describes-his-philosophy/), but it also applies to redistributionism. Which is definitely more benign, but nonetheless [produces bad results](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2015/09/02/redistribution-is-morally-dubious-economically-harmful-and-it-doesnt-work/).

The bottom line is that statism is a recipe for stagnation and [free markets are a route to prosperity](https://danieljmitchell.wordpress.com/2017/04/10/new-video-shows-the-simple-recipe-for-poor-nations-to-become-rich-nations-in-spite-of-bad-advice-from-international-bureaucracies/).
