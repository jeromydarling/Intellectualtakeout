After reading Charles Murray’s writings the last few days, my mind has been fixated on some of the [changes in America’s class structure and our (mis)perception of those changes](/blog/how-america-became-class-conscious).

It made me wonder: Where exactly do I fall in the class stratosphere? Are my current perceptions accurate or are they way off?

While Americans [like to think of themselves as middle class](http://www.gallup.com/poll/199727/americans-identification-middle-class-edges-back.aspx), it’s clear that [the U.S. middle class is shrinking](http://www.pewsocialtrends.org/2016/05/11/americas-shrinking-middle-class-a-close-look-at-changes-within-metropolitan-areas/) and has been for a long time.

As it happens, Pew Research Center [offers](http://www.pewresearch.org/fact-tank/2016/05/11/are-you-in-the-american-middle-class/) a feature that allows readers to calculate where they fall on the class index. It only takes about two minutes.
