Author Caro Clair Burke, who [wrote](https://www.instagram.com/reels/Df2l95Yt-NJ/) the viral novel “Yesteryear” about a “trad wife” who finds herself transported to a fictional past where she is abused by an overbearing husband, recently gave an interview for Time Magazine where she addressed anti-feminist concerns about her book.

“It was a woman who came to my Instagram account and asked me directly if she would like my novel if she was anti-feminist,” Burke said. “And the response I had was, if you’re reading books by women, you are a feminist.”

This is, of course, complete nonsense. Yet feminists, sensing that their ideology is losing popularity, are resorting to this debate tactic continually of late. Outspoken anti-feminists are told that the only reason they aren’t forced to keep quiet about their views is because of feminism. College students are told the only reason they are allowed an education is because of feminism. Wives are told the only reason they aren’t abused in their marriages is because of feminism. And on it goes.

Only a very narrow [definition of feminism](/2026/10/man-hating-feminists-have-sons/) would make these statements true. In reality, the reason it is culturally unacceptable to refuse women an education, to remove their control of their own brains and bodies, and to abuse them is more due to Western ideals and Christian influence than feminism. Muslim countries in the Middle East aren’t so kind to women.

“Feminism” for our purposes is still a rotten ideology. The most rotten of its many legacies is abortion. Estimates find that more than 66 million babies have been killed through abortion since _Roe v. Wade_. Under feminism’s [influence](https://www.kff.org/womens-health-policy/women-voters-revisited-inflation-abortion-and-increased-motivation-in-the-2024-election-countdown/), abortion is now the most important voting issue for women under 30. That means it can’t be dismissed as a fringe issue. If we’re talking about feminism, then we also need to talk about what feminism does to babies.

It’s a common debate tactic to make someone think they already [believe something they say they don’t](/2026/05/americans-morally-bankrupt/). Thus, if you want to close the nation’s borders, then you are sure to be reminded that you enjoy your local Mexican restaurant. If you want to combat job offshoring, then you will be reminded how many of your favorite kitchen appliances are “made in China.” And if you criticize feminism, someone’s waiting to tell you that it is the only thing standing between you and Sharia Law. (Ironically enough, feminists love to support Islam.)

Until feminists are just as concerned about protecting the unborn as protecting their right to kill the unborn, feminism will continue losing popularity. While [abortion is hardly the only reason](/2023/10/corporate-feminism-abortion/) to disavow feminism, it is the most important reason. The fact that abortion is such a foundational principle of feminism proves that the ideology itself is morally hollow and evil. It revolves around seeking female autonomy at all costs. It is so focused on making society androgynous, it will pursue that end even if it means killing the tiny humans who are a constant reminder that women and men are different.

So no, reading female authors doesn’t make anyone a feminist. We cannot be hoodwinked into accepting a murderous ideology by high-profile feminists pretending the definition of feminism is simply believing extremely mundane and uncontroversial facts about women, such as the fact that they can write books.
